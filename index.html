WHO AM I

An ECE undergraduate at MIT Manipal who got hooked on engineering the way most people get hooked on a family recipe.

My father, a technically enthusiastic engineer at heart, befriended me with math and machines before I knew what to do with either. Somewhere along the way, "curious kid" turned into someone who writes RTL in Verilog/VHDL, designs analog circuits in Cadence Virtuoso, and lives in Xilinx Vivado and MATLAB/Simulink long enough to build a Costas Loop, a BPSK modulator, and a two-stage CMOS amplifier.

When I'm not sitting and fixing timing constraints in Tcl, you'll find me writing a theatre script, buried in a finance case study, or rewatching a Tarantino or Scorsese film. Beyond everything, I want to make chips for a living.

Relevant coursework — Digital Design, VLSI, Hardware for Machine Learning, Analog Electronic Circuits, Computer Architecture
